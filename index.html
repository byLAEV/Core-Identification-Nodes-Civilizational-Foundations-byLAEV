01 / THE INITIATIVE

A framework for thinking about civilizational infrastructure.

Core Identification Nodes byLAEV is a research and development initiative dedicated to exploring the philosophical, theoretical, conceptual, methodological and technological foundations of a decentralized ecosystem of individual nodes.

The project examines how human events, histories, peer-to-peer communication and distributed ledgers may participate in shared civilizational infrastructure. It approaches registration, recognition and historical continuity as interconnected questions of social organization — not merely as isolated administrative or technological challenges.

Its foundations are developed progressively: from the definition of the problem and its premises to formal concepts, methods, protocols, architecture and implementation.

04 / RESEARCH PRINCIPLE

From foundational reasoning to verifiable development.

Every assertion incorporated into the project must be examined according to its definition, foundation, coherence, scope and verification conditions.

  • Distinguish axioms, definitions, premises and deductions.
  • Keep empirical hypotheses separate from established findings.
  • Make reasoning traceable and open to justified correction.
  • Develop technological proposals without presenting them as proven universal necessities.
